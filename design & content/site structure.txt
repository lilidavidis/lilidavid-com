What each page should do
Home

This is the page we’ve already outlined.
Its job is to:

express your core positioning
help visitors recognize their problem
show your approach
lead them toward contact
How I help

This page goes deeper into:

Facilitation
Consulting / advisory
Coaching

Its job is to help people understand:

what each service is for
when they might need it
what working with you looks like
About

This page should build trust and connection.
Its job is to answer:

who you are
what shaped your perspective
why you do this work
what informs your approach
Contact

This page should be very simple.
Its job is to make it easy to:

send a message
schedule a conversation
connect on LinkedIn
My recommendation

Keep the navigation exactly that simple.

Writing / Resources

Testimonials / Case Studies
