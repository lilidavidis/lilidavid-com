2. The problem: where organizations lose balance
Many organizations get stuck at one of two extremes

Inclusive in intention, but lacking clarity and traction
The group cares about participation, values, and involving people well, but roles and responsibilities are fuzzy, meetings are draining, progress is slow, and important things fall between the cracks.

Focused on performance, but low in ownership and engagement
The organization is trying to be efficient and get things done, but too much depends on leaders, decisions lack real buy-in, tensions grow between leaders and others, and people disengage or leave.

3. The bridge: what it takes to have the best of both

When inclusion is strong but clarity is weak, what is often needed is more structure: clearer roles and responsibilities, better decision-making processes, stronger meeting design, clearer priorities, and agreements that help things move without becoming rigid.

When performance is prioritized but ownership is weak, what is often needed is more participation: healthier communication patterns, more involvement in decisions, clearer expectations, stronger trust, more shared responsibility, and more sustainable leadership patterns.

The point is not to swing from one side to the other.
It is to build the structures and habits that allow effectiveness and humanity to reinforce each other.

4. How I help
Facilitation

I support groups in designing and running meetings that are purposeful, well-structured, and effective. If you can tell me what you want the meeting to achieve, I can help design the agenda and guide the group in a way that makes those outcomes more likely.

Consulting / advisory

I work more deeply with organizations that want to understand and address the core issues behind recurring challenges. This usually involves getting to know the context better, speaking with more than the initial sponsor, and identifying the patterns, structures, or dynamics that are driving the problem. From there, I can help shape practical responses and support implementation without taking over ownership.

Coaching

I support leaders and teams who want to better understand how their own behaviour influences team dynamics and outcomes. This can happen one-to-one or in a group setting. It is deeper personal and interpersonal work that helps people shift long-formed habits and develop more effective ways of relating, leading, and collaborating.

Framing line:
Across all three, I bring a systemic lens and pay attention to the patterns and conditions that shape how people work together.

6. Short bio / credibility

I’m a facilitator, consultant, and coach who supports organizations in building ways of working that are both effective and humane. My work focuses on the patterns, structures, and interactions that shape collaboration — helping teams create more clarity, shared responsibility, and progress with less friction.

My route into this work began in socially oriented and nonprofit projects, where I saw how often passion, care, and good intentions were undermined by unhealthy dynamics, fuzzy roles, and endless meetings with little traction. Discovering sociocracy in 2014 gave me a practical answer to a question I had long cared about: how can people work in ways that are both inclusive and effective? Since then, as a co-developer of Sociocracy 3.0, I’ve been supporting people worldwide through training, mentoring, and facilitation. Today I work mainly with individuals and smaller groups, often in SaaS and other for-profit companies, who want to share power and responsibility in ways that genuinely work.
